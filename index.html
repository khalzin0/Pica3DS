Pica3DS

A 3DS emulator that runs in your browser. Bring your own decrypted games and homebrew.

Drop a game anywhere on this page, or
.3ds.cci.cxi.cia.3dsx.elf

Games must be decrypted on your own console (this includes .cia files). Encrypted dumps are not supported.

Play it like an app: tap Share then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works offline.

System files

        
System files: none

        
Mii data, fonts and the bad word list have built-in stand-ins. Real files dumped and decrypted from your own 3DS (GodMode9) can be added here: system data, built-in apps (System Settings, Mii Maker…) and the HOME Menu (.app or .cia).

        

          
          
          
        

      

    


    

      

        
Save states

        
A snapshot of the whole game at this moment: load it to carry on from exactly here. Kept in this browser, per game. Keyboard: Shift+F1–F4 saves, F1–F4 loads.

        

        

          
          
          
        

      

      

        
Game saves

        
What the game saves itself (save data, extra data, SD card files) is kept in this browser automatically and comes back the next time you open the game.
