Log

      

    


    
Runs homebrew built with devkitARM/libctru, as .3dsx/.elf or packaged as a decrypted .3ds/.cxi (RomFS supported). Encrypted .3ds and .cia files are not supported. The 3D GPU (PICA200) is emulated in software (geometry shaders, procedural textures and gas are not yet). Games that read system data (Mii data, fonts) need those files dumped and decrypted from your own 3DS, added with "System files…". Commercial games are experimental and may stop on unsupported features.
